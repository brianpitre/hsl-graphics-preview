The full map lays the three screens side by side at their native resolution, as if they stood in one plane. A 560 px air gap with no LED sits between Screen 1 and each side screen: the 5′-8½″ in the working layout. Both riser wraps sit in a strip centered under Screen 1, each wrap reading left to right as seen from the house. The risers are ROE JT3.1 500 mm half panels at 160 px each: ten across each front, five on each end. The red box on Screen 1 is what the center camera takes in at its default shot. The dashed yellow line on each screen is 6′ above the deck, where all three screens stand. Files you load stay in this browser.

Trusses, Feb 2027 rig

Positions come from the top-down rigging sketch, with everything upstage of the beam moved 1′ downstage on 17 Oct. The 70′ Screen 1 truss rides just above the screen and moves with Screen 1’s sliders. The others open at the trims set on 15 Oct. Each slider sets one pick point’s trim, the bottom of the truss above the deck. Uneven points tilt the truss without stretching it, so its ends swing in and the chains lean slightly; two points can differ by no more than their spacing along the truss. Green dots are the 600 lb hang points.
